# Multi-section document qualification review — 2026-10-30

On-demand qualification suggestions now process up to eight sequential 6,000-byte sections with 512-byte overlap. Overlap helps avoid splitting a short quotation at a section boundary. Every returned excerpt must occur exactly in its source section, duplicates are removed, and at most 48 distinct excerpts are returned. A section can return at most 12 excerpts; reaching that limit and omitting excerpts at the overall cap are reported. A failed section is identified in the coverage count, other sections may continue, and a runtime failure stops the run with a visible reason. Remaining text beyond the section budget is explicitly marked unattempted. Byte progress is an attempted input frontier, not proof that the model received or understood every byte. One completed result is retained for a lost-response retry until another result replaces it.

Deterministic fixtures covered an exact excerpt crossing a section boundary, a failed first section with a valid later section, Unicode boundaries, the eight-section limit, the 48-excerpt cap and result replay. The [local-model fixture](document-multisection-live-2026-10-30.txt) used fictional text and `qwen3.5:9b` digest `c97eb11d70b1acdc88af01eef566c1fe4f7fbe93eb1afc06871132f293ff425a`, fully GPU-resident with a 4,096-token context. It proposed a fictional certification after the first 6,000-byte section and returned only exact source excerpts; two sections succeeded in 0.98 seconds. Sampled system RAM was approximately 22.9 GiB free and dedicated GPU use approximately 10.7 GiB; these are not peak measurements.

A rendered local UI fixture exercised progress, an unattempted remainder, a failed section and selection of one fictional excerpt using a mocked API. This is not a broad document benchmark: the model can miss qualifications, return incomplete excerpts, misinterpret the source, or fail on other documents and layouts. Candidates must review all suggestions and the full source. No document was sent externally or application submitted.
