# Local model document and form checks — 2026-11-01

The installed `qwen3.5:9b` model (digest prefix `c97eb11d70b1`) was started through Waypoint's resource gate with a 4,096-token context. It was the only resident model and Ollama reported it fully GPU-resident. A pre-start point-in-time sample showed approximately 25.4 GiB free system memory and 5.8 GiB dedicated GPU use; a later sample while resident showed 20.4 GiB free and 12.4 GiB dedicated use. These are snapshots, not peak measurements. The model was unloaded after the checks.

The fictional two-page PDF overlap fixture passed three runs of the production document-vision test. In a captured result, the model flagged the overprinted second page, but described the overlapping words as “Distributed systems manager,” which is not either of the two original strings. It also made advisory content judgments on the clean first page. The independent text-geometry check reported 29 overlapping pairs on page two and none on page one. This small fixture does not establish general visual accuracy or reliable transcription.

One fictional English form passed the local-model mapping, prefill and visual-transcription pipeline. The German multilingual fixture failed twice with the original prompt because the model placed the ordinary given-name field in manual review; it was not filled. A prompt revision passed the German fixture twice but over-flagged the Portuguese email field in two runs, while the original prompt passed that Portuguese fixture once. A schema-constrained variant produced both a mapping and a manual-review flag for the German field and was rejected by the application. Both changes were discarded; disagreement remains a manual-review case.

In two runs of a fictional 20-field fixture, the model flagged a Portuguese work-eligibility question for manual review rather than mapping it to an email address. In one separate visual fixture, it transcribed an empty email placeholder as an entered value; the independent live-value check marked that comparison unconfirmed. These results are limited fictional examples, not a representative accuracy or repeatability benchmark. No employer site or application submission was involved.

## Mixed-language ambiguity checks — 2026-11-01

With the same resource-gated `qwen3.5:9b` model and 4,096-token context, a fictional English ambiguous-contact / Spanish eligibility / French email fixture passed three sequential runs. A separate fictional Spanish ambiguous-contact / French sponsorship / English email fixture passed two sequential runs. Both deliberately attach an email autocomplete hint to the eligibility or sponsorship question; the production suggestion path left that question and the ambiguous contact question for manual review while mapping the ordinary email. This is a small deterministic-prompt sample, not an accuracy estimate, and the earlier German false positive remains unresolved.

While the model was resident, one sample showed approximately 20.2 GiB free memory and 12.3 GiB dedicated GPU use; these are snapshots, not peaks. The model was unloaded after the checks.
