mapper instead of its separate exact-label mapper. Proposals bind the bundled
fictional profile and original native schema hashes. Captured-frame selectors
receive collision-checked opaque aliases; named section labels and their
inferred flags are model context, while native section identities stay private.
Candidate values are not needed in the initial key-mapping prompt.

Existing native option resolution, length/format constraints, sensitivity,
credential and model-review exclusions still apply. The execution-specific
preview refuses structural errors rather than converting them to exact-label
actions. Failed model batches retain manual exclusions and warnings. No model
proposal grants authority. Each attempted entry still requires current native
identity and independent before/after visual checks. This slice covers contact
profile keys; arbitrary reviewed facts, repeated histories and broader workflow
planning remain unfinished.

The saved-record interface distinguishes contact proposals from verified entries
and retains bounded review notes. It does not call a proposal successful entry.

## Native loading failure and repair

The first frozen fictional French form inventory contained nine native controls:
seven answerable contact/choice fields and two unknowns (salary and preferred
contact channel). Its first rehearsal stopped before any semantic proposal or
entry because the loading guard did not see two unchanged comparisons.

A separate native-only probe measured the same saved bytes. Opening took
6,397 ms; three subsequent inspections took 2,901, 2,889 and 2,893 ms. All four
schemas were identical. The old three-second observation window admitted only
one comparison: the trace recorded two snapshots and one unchanged comparison.
Fresh inspections had no review barrier. This was a budget failure, not evidence
of a changing form or a model mistake.

The observation admission window now uses three measured inspection durations
plus two seconds, clamped to 3-30 seconds and anchored to the original start.
There are still at most three comparisons, and two consecutive unchanged,
nonempty comparisons remain mandatory. Disappearing fields and challenge walls
remain visible. The cap controls admission; it does not interrupt an in-flight
CDP call or establish a complete native-operation cancellation deadline.

The repaired native-only probe on the original immutable archive opened in
10,008 ms. Its trace recorded three snapshots, two unchanged comparisons,
2,892 ms slowest inspection and a 10,678 ms admission window. All four schemas
again matched, with no walls or independent-entry barriers. The browser closed;
the replay reported zero cached non-read replies and unmanaged child sessions.
