# Typed qualification quantities — 14 November 2026

## Change and boundaries

The offline qualification actor can now propose a bare numeric value when a
text question carries the quantity's unit. The earlier prose guard correctly
refused bare `4` against `4 years`; it remains unchanged. A separate typed path
retains the exact selected fact, numeric literal, unit, subject, native question
identity and source hash. It reuses bounded fact selection, model budgets,
immutable qualification archives, source-revision cancellation and native/visual
entry checks. Ordinary answer/document drafting does not use this new path.

The local model classifies the requested answer shape and proposes an extraction.
Names and prose continue through drafting and natural-wording review. For a
quantity, local checks require an exact ASCII scalar, one source quantity, a
recognized matching question unit and a specific shared subject. Competing
quantities for that subject in the entire staged reviewed-fact pool force review,
even when not selected as the citation. The separate question reviewer also sees
the bounded selected context. Source/field/selection bindings are recomputed
before entry. A typed proposal cannot overwrite or mask a failed prose review.

Bounds, ranges, negation, approximation, conversions, written-number conversion,
missing units and uncertain relationships remain manual. The conservative
shared-subject check does not establish semantic equivalence, applicant identity,
period or scope; the independent model question check still vetoes entry. Model
agreement never independently verifies a qualification or authorizes an action.
The measured actor scope remains text/text-area questions, not native number,
select, repeated-row or whole-workflow qualification.
