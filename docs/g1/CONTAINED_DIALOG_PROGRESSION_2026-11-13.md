## Mechanism

The browser can inventory one rendered semantic dialog, expose bounded opaque
control IDs to the local model and refuse ambiguous, unavailable or form-submit
controls. The model proposes an optional-overlay action or review. Its response
cannot create an approval or authorize a live action. Legal consent, optional
tracking opt-in, account/challenge actions and submission are excluded by the
advisory contract; malformed advice remains manual.

A native witness binds the document, form, dialog and input-value digest. These
witnesses are rechecked around target images and local visual review. The click
guard revalidates the target label and enclosing dialog after hover. After the
guarded headless action, document/value changes, new replay gaps and persistent
dialogs stop progression. A sealed closure
capture must survive a fresh native check after blind post-action visual review.
The actor exists only on the isolated recorded-browser path, before any candidate
field is entered, with a two-record global budget. It does not dismiss DOM nodes
through injected page edits or relax the existing live click guard.

Records retain the proposal, target/detail images, closure image, visual result,
refusal and follow-up view. A missing follow-up capture stops further entry. The
web view distinguishes native/visual closure from suggestions, names the updated
view and exposes an explicit accessible view selector. Shared image/hash and
button mechanisms are reused. Oversized document Accept input is also rejected
before allocating its split list; accepted negotiation behavior is unchanged.
